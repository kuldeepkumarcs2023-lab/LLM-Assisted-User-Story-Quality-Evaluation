# Project Differentiation

The project is designed as an explainable requirements-quality workbench rather than a simple score calculator.

## Advanced modules
- INVEST evaluation with explainable scoring
- Requirement smell and risk detection
- Story Quality DNA and confidence signals
- Revision comparison and quality movement
- Batch quality experiment
- Requirement Studio with story slicing and traceability matrix
- Acceptance criteria generation
- History, analytics, exports and human-review guidance

These features are intended as a distinctive academic implementation. They do not establish that no similar system exists worldwide.
